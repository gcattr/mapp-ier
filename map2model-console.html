Want the 3D file instead?

This page is for ordering a printed tile from the shop — you pick the place and the colours, and it arrives finished. If you have a 3D printer at home and would rather make one yourself, these do a similar job and hand you a file:

  • MiniSkyline City skylines as a printable block.
  • TerraPrinter Terrain tiles from anywhere on earth.
  • TrailPrint3D Hikes and trails as 3D terrain.
  • Topo Trail Topographic route models.
  • TouchTerrain Free, from Iowa State — terrain only, very configurable.
  • Touch Mapper Free tactile street maps, built for accessibility.
  • Terrain2STL Free and quick, terrain elevation only.
  • Blosm for Blender A Blender add-on. The most control, and the steepest learning curve.

Not affiliated with any of them, and none of them are checked or endorsed here — they are just the tools that come up when people ask. Buildings, terrain and water vary a lot between them.
